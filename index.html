About

I am a computational linguist based in São Paulo, Brazil. I combine computational linguistics, NLP engineering, and systematic AI evaluation to develop more reliable language technologies.

I have a PhD in Linguistics from the University of São Paulo, with research focused on Brazilian Portuguese phonology. My professional background spans NLU, NLG, ASR, and TTS development, language data, and AI quality assurance.
